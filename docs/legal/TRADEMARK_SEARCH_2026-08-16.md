# Trimlet name and trademark search record

- Checked: 2026-08-16
- Scope: preliminary engineering review for repository naming
- Result: acceptable to continue using Trimlet as the GitHub project name; not a legal clearance for commercial launch or registration

## Searches performed

### General web and software searches

Exact-name searches combined with video editor, macOS, Windows, software, app, and GitHub terms did not identify a prominent video-editing product named Trimlet. Results were mainly an unrelated health/fitness product, surname references, and ordinary trimming language.

### Japan — J-PlatPat

Official search: https://www.j-platpat.inpit.go.jp/t0100

- `Trimlet`, trade-mark search field, exact match, application/registration records: 0 results.
- `トリムレット`, similar-pronunciation search: 46 broad candidates across classes.
- The pronunciation candidates include `DigitalTriplet / デジタルトリプレット`, registration 6374829, with class 9 among several classes.
- No exact `Trimlet` software mark was found in this search.

The similar-pronunciation result is intentionally recorded because an exact-name-only search is insufficient. `DigitalTriplet` is visually and phonetically distinguishable, but its class overlap makes it a candidate for professional review before a Japanese commercial filing.

### United States — USPTO Trademark Search

Official search: https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/search/

- Wordmark `Trimlet`, with Live and Dead records enabled: 0 results.

### Multi-jurisdiction — TMview

Official search: https://www.tmdn.org/tmview/

- Partial-match search for `Trimlet`: 1 result in the participating-office dataset at the time of search.
- Candidate: Canadian application 534193-00 / registration TMA327239, a figurative mark in class 7 for personal exercise equipment.
- Status shown by TMview: expired, with an expiry date of 2002-12-19.
- No active software/video-editor result was shown for the literal partial-match query.

TMview itself states that it is not an official register and its information has no legal effect. The originating national register remains authoritative.

## Interpretation

No exact active mark for a video-editing application was identified in the checked sources. The name can remain in the source repository and development documentation.

This review does not establish non-infringement or registrability because:

- similarity is assessed by appearance, sound, meaning, goods/services, territory, and earlier rights;
- unregistered and well-known rights may exist;
- records change after the search date;
- the exact commercial goods/services wording has not been drafted;
- Canada and other national source registers were not individually searched beyond TMview.

Before paid distribution, significant marketing spend, or a trademark filing, have a qualified professional review at least classes 9 and 42 and the `DigitalTriplet` candidate in Japan. Re-run the searches immediately before filing or launch.
